    — HOW WE PROTECT YOUR PEOPLE

    Prevent. Respond. Recover.

    01

    Prevent

    The best defense is stopping attacks before they reach your users. AI-driven anti-phishing detects and quarantines suspicious email. Security awareness training and phishing tests help your team spot what slips through, and dark web monitoring alerts us when stolen credentials tied to your domain appear.

    02

    Respond

    Cloud detection and response watches your SaaS applications like Microsoft 365 and Salesforce around the clock. Machine learning analyzes user behavior, and when something unusual happens — like an unauthorized login — we're notified instantly and shut it down.

    03

    Recover

    Microsoft and Google keep their apps running, but protecting your data is your responsibility. Automated backups three times a day protect email, OneDrive, SharePoint, Teams and Google Drive, with point-in-time and granular restores when you need them.

    — USER PROTECTION FAQ

    User protection, explained

    Have another question? Call 865‑332‑0008.

    Doesn't Microsoft back up my Microsoft 365 data?

    Microsoft and Google guarantee their applications stay available, but you're responsible for protecting your data from ransomware, user mistakes and other threats. Our SaaS backup runs three times a day with flexible restore options.

    What is security awareness training?

    Short, practical training that teaches your employees to recognize and avoid phishing emails and other scams. We pair it with simulated phishing tests so you can see who needs extra help.

    What is dark web monitoring?

    We monitor the dark web for stolen login information tied to your company's email domains and alert you when a compromise is found, so passwords can be changed before it turns into a data breach.
